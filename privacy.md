# Privacy Policy – Mailbox Pulse

**Last updated: 8 October 2026**

Mailbox Pulse is a read-only application that provides a simple overview of your Gmail mailbox activity while protecting your privacy.

**What we access**

Mailbox Pulse reads only email headers and labels, such as sender, subject, date and label information. It never accesses or reads email bodies, message previews or attachments.

**Read-only access**

Mailbox Pulse cannot send, edit, delete or modify your emails or mailbox settings.

**Data storage and security**

All mailbox data and Google authentication tokens remain stored locally on your laptop. Nothing is uploaded to external servers or shared with third parties.

**Data sharing**

Mailbox Pulse does not collect, transmit, sell or share your information with anyone.

**Revoking access**

You can revoke Mailbox Pulse's access to your Gmail account at any time through your Google Account settings:

[Google Account – Third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections)

Revoking access prevents Mailbox Pulse from accessing your Gmail account until you authorise it again.

**Your privacy**

Mailbox Pulse is designed to operate entirely on your laptop. Your mailbox information stays under your control.
